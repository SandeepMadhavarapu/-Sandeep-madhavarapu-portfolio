02 / HOW I THINK

The work
beneath
the work.

Visual polish draws you in. Engineering depth is what makes a product worth trusting.

01

Model the real problem.

Understand constraints, threat models, edge cases and failure paths before deciding what the interface should promise.

02

Make complexity legible.

Turn system behavior into interfaces, observability and documentation that help people understand why a result exists.

03

Test the uncomfortable paths.

Care about duplicates, invalid states, inaccessible interactions, poor connectivity and assumptions that do not survive real inputs.

04

Show the evidence.

Separate prototypes from production systems. Link to code, document limitations and make technical claims inspectable.
